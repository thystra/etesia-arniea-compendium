# Resurrection Diamonds

<!-- /home/alan/src/etesia-arniea-compendium/docs/items/resurrection-diamonds.md -->

Module version: **0.1.4**

## Established campaign rule

Ordinary diamonds cannot power resurrection magic. Resurrection spells require purpose-made **resurrection diamonds**, which can be awarded, traded, stolen, controlled, or withheld by temples, factions, nobles, and states.

The campaign has already established:

- **Revivify:** consumes one standard Resurrection Diamond.
- **Resurrection:** has previously consumed two standard Resurrection Diamonds.

The item quantity is tracked separately from the spell's daily use. Recovering a spell use at dawn never restores a consumed stone.

## Grade framework

The compendium includes four portable item grades:

1. **Resurrection Diamond** — standard grade; established for Revivify.
2. **Consecrated Resurrection Diamond** — temple-prepared higher grade.
3. **Sovereign Resurrection Diamond** — major institutional or state-controlled grade.
4. **Perfect Resurrection Diamond** — exceptional top grade for the rarest rites.

Only the standard Revivify cost is mechanically settled by this release. Higher-grade exchange rates remain provisional. One candidate under consideration is:

- **Raise Dead:** two standard Resurrection Diamonds **or** one Consecrated Resurrection Diamond.

This candidate is included as a GM-facing note, not as an adopted rule. The Sovereign and Perfect grades intentionally have no fixed spell equivalencies yet.

## Foundry usage

- Drag a grade from **Etesia Items** to a party or NPC inventory.
- Adjust item quantity to match stones actually held.
- Manually reduce the relevant quantity when a resurrection spell succeeds.
- Harald's Keywell actor begins with one standard Resurrection Diamond.
