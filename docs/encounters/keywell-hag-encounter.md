# Keywell Hag Encounter

<!-- /home/alan/src/etesia-arniea-compendium/docs/encounters/keywell-hag-encounter.md -->

Module version: **0.1.2**

## Intended party

- Zari, level 8 Soul Blade Warlock
- Zyphyr, level 8 Fiend Warlock
- AIDAN, level 8 Warforged Paladin
- Harald, NPC healer and civilian escort

The encounter is intended as a moderate-to-challenging finale for a reduced party. It deliberately mixes Constitution, Wisdom, Strength, and Dexterity saves because AIDAN's AC 23 and Zyphyr's *Shadow of Moil* make attack-only encounters unreliable.

## Hook

Near dusk, Edric Vale's riderless horse walks into Keywell. Its saddle and flanks are bloodied. Edric left that morning after hearing that Mara Vale, a child missing for seven years, had been seen on the old forest road. Mara still appears eight years old and wears the clothes in which she vanished.

## Truth

Mother Sallow abducted Mara into a shallow border between Etesia and the Feywild. Only a few days passed for Mara. When Edric found her and promised to bring her home, Mother Sallow treated the promise as acceptance of a debt for seven years of hospitality.

## Pack contents

- **Mother Sallow, Keeper of Lost Years** - mobile save-based controller boss
- **Hollowoak Guardian** - durable space-control defender
- **Memory Bundle** - place three copies as destructible encounter objectives
- **Edric Vale, Wounded Woodsman** - stable at 0 HP, restrained, and under Borrowed Time
- **Mara Vale, the Unaged Child** - noncombatant witness and rescue objective
- **Harald, Keywell Field Healer** - encounter-specific support copy with dawn-recovery healing, Sanctuary, Prayer of Healing, Revivify, breastplate, shield, and one Resurrection Diamond
- **Edric's Bloodied Horse** - opening hook and clue carrier

## Finale setup

Place Mother Sallow within 10 feet of the Hollowoak Guardian. Place three Memory Bundle tokens around the cottage or hollow. Edric begins at 0 HP, stable, unconscious, prone, and restrained. Mara begins near Edric or a boundary stone.

### Memory Bundle sequence

1. First opened or destroyed: remove one unused Legendary Resistance and refresh The Weight of Memory.
2. Second: disable Step Between Stories.
3. Third: disable Magic Resistance, restore Mara's memories, and end Mother Sallow's claim over Edric.

Each bundle has AC 15, 15 HP, immunity to Poison and Psychic damage, and vulnerability to Fire and Slashing damage. A character can instead use an action and succeed on a DC 15 Arcana, Religion, or Sleight of Hand check to open one safely.

## Harald and Edric

Healing magic works normally on Edric.

- Before danger, Harald may use *Aid* on Zari, Zyphyr, and AIDAN.
- Round 1: Harald casts *Healing Word* on Edric and moves toward him.
- Round 2: Harald casts *Sanctuary* on Edric or Mara and positions to free Edric.
- Round 3: Harald frees or escorts Edric and Mara toward cover or the boundary stone.
- Later: Harald uses emergency healing if a hero falls, but otherwise guards the civilians.

Harald carries one standard Resurrection Diamond. His *Revivify* use returns at dawn, but a consumed stone never does. While Borrowed Time persists, Edric's Speed is 10 feet, he has Disadvantage on Strength and Dexterity checks and saves, cannot take Reactions, can take either an Action or Bonus Action but not both, and cannot regain hit points above 12.

## Mother Sallow tactics

- Open with The Weight of Memory if it can affect at least two heroes.
- Use Borrowed Years on a concentrating warlock or the current primary damage dealer.
- Use Roots of the Broken Promise to divide the battlefield and expose the bundles.
- Let AIDAN's AC matter: the guardian can engage him while Mother Sallow pressures other defenses.
- At 30 HP or fewer, Mother Sallow offers Edric and Mara in exchange for safe passage.

## Difficulty controls

For the intended moderate version, use Mother Sallow at 125 HP and the guardian at 95 HP. If the party is depleted, reduce the guardian to 70 HP and remove Twist the Bargain. If the party is fully rested and dominating, allow Mother Sallow a second use of The Weight of Memory after the second bundle opens.
