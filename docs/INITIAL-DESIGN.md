# Initial Mechanical Design Notes

The campaign sources specify roles, themes, approximate character-level inspirations, and encounter needs but do not provide complete stat blocks for most NPCs. The initial actor mechanics are therefore original playtest designs.

## Encounter goals

- Every combatant intended to threaten the current party has at least +5 to hit.
- Every encounter family includes saving-throw pressure so high Armor Class and imposed disadvantage do not shut down all opposition.
- Standard humanoid roles are scaled upward before introducing exotic creatures.
- Vicar Kolokai Sanguis and Lady Amara Grethry are built as cohort-supported bosses rather than isolated solo encounters.
- War Golems are military constructs; Warforged personhood is a distinct question.

## Suggested first test encounters

- **Patrol:** 1 SSP Sergeant, 2 Soldier Mk I, 1 Archer Mk II, 1 Forge Cleric.
- **Veteran column:** 1 Sergeant, 3 Soldier Mk II, 2 Archer Mk II, 1 Forge Cleric.
- **Recovered facility:** 2 War Golem Mk I, 1 Spider Defense Unit, environmental hazards.
- **Modern production unit:** 1 War Golem Mk II, 2 Spider Defense Units, 2 SSP Soldier Mk II.
- **Vicar cohort:** Vicar Sanguis, 1 Forge Cleric, 1 Sergeant, 4 mixed soldiers; add undead appropriate to the scene.
- **Amara strike team:** Lady Amara, 2 Archer Mk II, 2 Soldier Mk II, and a Mage Mk II or III.

These are starting points, not encounter-balance guarantees.
